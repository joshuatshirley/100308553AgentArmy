Appendix C
Officer Uniform Requirements
C–1.  General
Officers are responsible for procuring and maintaining uniforms appropriate to their assigned duties. Officers will
procure and maintain sufficient quantities of personal items necessary to ensure acceptable standards of personal hy-
giene and appearance. It is mandatory that all officers dress according to their position as an officer of the U.S. Army
and according to the traditions and customs of the Service.

Table C – 1 lists the major items of uniform clothing that are normally prescribed by commanders with the minimum

C – 3.  Accessories
Officers also are responsible for procuring and maintaining adequate quantities of appropriate accessories, insignia,
footwear, undergarments, headgear, and gloves for use with the uniforms in table C – 1.
