## Summary. This pamphlet describes the

criteria and procedures for the Army Re-
tention Program. For those Soldiers serv-
ing in the Regular Army, it outlines pro-
cedures for immediate continued service.
For Soldiers separating from the Regular
Army, it provides eligibility criteria and
options for enlistment or transfer into the
Reserve Component (U.S. Army Reserve

## and Army National Guard).
