Preferral
The action taken when an accuser is sworn to charges, by a commissioned officer of the armed forces authorized
to administer oaths, that the accuser has personal knowledge of the charges set forth in the charge sheet or has
investigated the same and that they are true in fact to the best of the accuser's knowledge and belief.  The accuser
then signs the DD Form 458 in block 11d.
