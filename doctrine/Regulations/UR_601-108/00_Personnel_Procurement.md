## SUMMARY of CHANGE

USAREC Regulation 601-108
Army Chaplain Recruiting Program
This major revision, dated 1 Jan 2015 contains the following changes:
o Major changes in organization to align with processing of applicants.
o Revision of policies and procedures to account for electronic processing.
o Added duties of Officer In Charge.
o Removal of duplication and excerpts from existing Army regulations and policies.
o Ch 2 Processing changed to include the entire chaplain recruiting program.
o 2-1 Application process updated and moved to para 2-9.
o 2-1 Mission added.
o 2-2 Medical processing updated and moved to para 2-11.
o 2-2 Recruiter zones defined.
o 2-3 Applicant interview updated to Senior Army Chaplain Interview and moved to para 2-12.
o 2-3 Prospecting added.
o 2-4 Board packet process and procedures updated and moved to para 2-13.
o 2-4 School programs added.
o 2-5 Waiver processing updated and moved to para 2-10.
o 2-5 Scheduling appointments added.
o 2-6 Selection and non-selection updated and moved to para 2-14.
o 2-6 Operations management added.
o 2-7 Situational Awareness Brief added.
o 2-8 Mission planning added.
o Ch 3 Future Chaplains updated and moved to para 2-15.
o 3-1 Follow-up, contact and mentor program included in para 2-15.
