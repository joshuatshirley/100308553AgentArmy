Appendix B
Format for Submitting Accession Incentives and Programs for Approval
B–1. Program proposal
The program proposal must be enclosed (second to last column) and must include—
a. A lengthier description of the program and any items that require further explanation.
b. A justification.
c. Performance measures.
d. Performance outcomes (for resubmissions).
e. A legal review.
f. Routing to show the chain of command approval.
g. The internal controls and other measures in place to limit potential for fraud, waste, or abuse.
h. The name(s) and contact information of the person(s) responsible for tracking such fraud, waste, or
abuse.
Note. Incidents of fraud, waste, or abuse must be reported to the ASA (M&RA), or their designee, immedi-
ately.
B–2. Program renewal
In addition, documents for programs being renewed must include the following:
a. The items in paragraph B – 1.
b. Information on the number of incidents of fraud, waste, or abuse during the previous year.
c. The measures taken to limit further opportunities for fraud, waste, or abuse.
d. Summaries of internal controls.
