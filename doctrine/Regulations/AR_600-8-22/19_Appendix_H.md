## Appendix H


## Badges and Tabs

H–1. Combat badges
Soldiers redeployed more than 12 months or reassigned to a command other than their wartime com-
mand must submit their request through command channels to Commanding General, U.S. Army Human
Resources Command (AHRC–PDP–A), 1600 Spearhead Division Avenue, Fort Knox, KY 40122–5408,
for processing. Eligible veterans and retirees may submit a request directly to the Commanding General,
U.S. Army Human Resources Command at the same address.
