## Summary of Change

AR 40–501
Standards of Medical Fitness
This administrative revision, dated 29 September 2025—
o
Updates paragraph 2 with the correct citation (para 2–4).
o
Replaces DoDI 6200.03 with DoDI 6200.06 (app A).
This administrative revision, dated 17 May 2025—
o
Replaces Army Combat Fitness Test with Army Fitness Test (para 1–4c(3)).
o
Replaces the acronym for Army Combat Fitness Test with the acronym for Army Fitness Test
(throughout).
This administrative revision, dated 17 March 2023—
o
Deletes paragraph 1–4, Associated publications, and realigns remaining paragraphs (chap 1).
o
Updates internal reference citations incorporating Army Directive 2016–28 (para 1–4c(1)).
o
Updates the internal reference citation clarifying various waiver authorities for Officer Candidate School
in-Service applicants and Warrant Officer Candidate School direct accessions (para 1–6j).
o
Updates the internal reference citation including the waiver authority for Army divers (para 1–6o).
o
Updates the internal reference citation adding Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act
information (para 1–7).
o
Updates paragraph citations (throughout).
This administrative revision, dated 13 December 2022—
o
DA Form 3349–SG (Physical Profile Record) will be prescribed in DA Pam 40–502.
o
Changes Army Physical Fitness Test to Army Combat Fitness Test (throughout).
This major revision, dated 27 June 2019—
o
Incorporates Army Directive 2016–28, (paras 1–4c(1) and 1–4c(2)).
o
Clarifies various waiver authorities for Officer Candidate School in-Service applicants and Warrant
Officer Candidate School direct accessions (paras 1–6k).
o
Includes the waiver authority for Army divers (para 1–6o).
o
Adds Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act information (para 1–7).

--- Page 3 (Original: p.3) ---

o
Implements DoDI 6130.03, Volume 1 by updating and realigning physical standards for enlistment,
appointment, and induction (chap 2).
o
Supersedes Army Directive 2012–23, Body Fat Standards for U.S. Army Enlisted Accessions, by
changing the maximum body fat standards for males and females in the 17–20 years of age categories
(tables 2–1 and 2–2).
o
Updates retention standards (chap 3).
o
Realigns chapters into similar order as to anatomical sites and body systems (chaps 3, 4, and 5).
o
Updates the stinging insect profiling language and policy (paras 3–3e(1) and 3–35a).
o
Updates cancer profiling language and policy (paras 3–3e(2)).
o
Clarifies the retention standard for hearing and the Military Operational Hearing Test, such as the Speech
Recognition in Noise Test (para 3–9a).
o
Adds a new retention standard for rheumatologic conditions (para 3–30).
o
Adds a new retention standard for sleep disorders (para 3–32).
o
Updates the psychiatric retention standards to reflect the most recent diagnoses changes in the Diagnostic
and Statistical Manual for Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition (para 3–33e).
o
Supersedes Army Directive 2013–12, Implementation of Department of Defense Policy Change
Concerning Chronic Adjustment Disorder (para 3–33e).
o
Removes a section on personality and other disorders that previously rendered an individual
administratively unfit (formerly para 3–35).
o
Adds a new section on conditions and circumstances not constituting a physical disability (para 3–36).
o
Implements Section 1177, Title 10, United States Code by requiring medical examinations in certain
instances prior to administrative separations under conditions other than honorable (para 3–37a).
o
Removes the Farnsworth Lantern as a valid flight color vision test and adds the Service approved computer
based color vision tests for aviation personnel as a valid color vision test (para 4–5a(4)(b)).
o
Adds medical standards for aircrew for unmanned aircraft systems (para 4–34).
o
Adds medical fitness standards for civil affairs and psychological operations initial training and retention
in the military occupational specialty (paras 5–4 and 5–5).
o
Adds initial selection and retention medical fitness standards for divers (para 5–11 and 5–12).
o
Adds initial selection and retention medical fitness standards for Army maritime sea duty (para 5–13).
o
Adds initial selection and retention medical fitness standards for small unmanned aircraft system operators
(para 5–14).
o
Moves aeromedical administration guidance to DA Pam 40–502 (formerly chap 6).
o
Moves physical profiling to AR 40–502 (formerly chap 7).

o
Moves individual medical readiness standards to AR 40–502 (formerly chap 11).

incorporates material in a reorganized format (throughout).

Army Directive 2016–28; and supersedes
Army Directive 2012–23.
Distribution. This publication is avail-

tended for the Regular Army, the Army
National Guard/Army National Guard of
the United States, and the U.S. Army Re-
serve.
