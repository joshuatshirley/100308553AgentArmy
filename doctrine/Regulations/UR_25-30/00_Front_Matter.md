## SUMMARY of CHANGE

USAREC Reg 25-30
USAREC Business Cards
This administrative revision, dated 30 September 2022
●    Streamlines the Multi-Selection Business Card Ordering Process.
●    Changes the number of printed business cards authorized per fiscal year.
●    Clarifies instructions for procurement of USAREC Business Cards at personal expense.
●     Adds the usage of an electronic contact card, and DISA-approved electronic business card for use on
Government Furnished Equipment (GFE), Apple iPhone.
●    Clarifies the responsibility of Leaders to inspect all electronic contact cards and business cards to
ensure their subordinates are meeting professional standards.
●    Clarifies policies prohibiting USAREC organizations from purchasing any form of printed business
cards and electronic business card solutions with organizational funds.
