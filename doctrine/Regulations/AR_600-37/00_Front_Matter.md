## SUMMARY of CHANGE

AR 600 – 37
Unfavorable Information
This regulation is certified current on 2 October 2020. Aside from the following administrative changes, no other
changes were made to certify the currency of this regulation—
o
Updates Department of the Army signature authority and history statement (title page).
o
Updates Uniform Code of Military Justice article number (para 3–4b(2)).
o
Updates Uniform Code of Military Justice nomenclature (para 3–4b(5)).
o
Updates titles of references and associated websites (app A).
o
Adds “United States Code” to the glossary.
