UR 10-1-1 – Brigade Organizations and Functions
4-1. Organization, Functions, and Designation of Responsibilities
The mission of the U.S. Army Recruiting Brigade Headquarters is as follows:
a. Commands the U.S. Army Recruiting Brigade, consisting of the headquarters and all U.S. Army
Recruiting Battalions located within assigned geographic boundaries, and exercises authority over all
assigned personnel.
b. Performs managerial, personnel, administrative, operational, training, budget, promotional, logistical,
automation, liaison and coordination functions needed to operate the Recruiting Brigade Headquarters and
subordinate activities.
c. Provides mission support for all Recruiting Brigade activities.
d. Supervises the RA and USAR programs and missions pertaining to recruitment and enlistment of
personnel.  Also, to assist, as directed, the ARNG, Army Medical Procurement, and other specialized
personnel procurement programs.

138 complaints; military personnel law; policies, plans, and directives review; line of duty investigations.
Provides assistance to, and coordinates with, the USAREC labor law counselor concerning all labor
law matters and issues within the Brigade.
Provides fiscal, procurement, and contract advice and assistance to the brigade.
Resolves legal problems regarding administrative boards, investigations, or other military tribunals.
Provides technical supervision and training of legal personnel in the brigade and its subordinate units.
Provides legal counsel to the CPO, EOA, and the brigade.
Serves as the Brigade ethics counselor.
Manages the brigade’s Financial Disclosure Management program.
Assists with litigation in which the United States has an interest.
Operates brigade legal training programs as required by Army Regulation.
