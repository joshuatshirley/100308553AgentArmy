Appendix C
Background Investigation Request Procedures
C–1.  General
DA background investigation requests are processed according to this regulation. To conserve investiga-
tive resources and to ensure that background investigations are limited to those essential to current oper-
ations and are clearly authorized by DA policies, organizations requesting investigations must ensure that
continuing command attention and oversight is given to the investigative request process.
C–2.  Limitations to background investigation request submissions
It is particularly important that the provision of EO 13526 requiring strict limitations on the dissemination of
official information and material be closely adhered to and limited to investigation requests for those that
have a clear need for access to classified information. Investigations required to determine eligibility for
appointment or retention in the DA, in either a civilian or military capacity, must not be requested in fre-
quency or scope exceeding that provided for in this regulation. Close observance of the guidelines in this
paragraph will allow the ISP to operate more efficiently and permit more effective, timely, and responsive
service in accomplishing investigations. The following guidelines have been developed to simplify and fa-
cilitate the investigative request process:
a.  Submit investigation requests in accordance with current policy issued by the DNI and Federal In-
vestigative Notices issued by the ISP.
b.  Limit requests for investigation to those that are essential to current operations and clearly author-
ized by DA policies and attempt to utilize individuals who, under the provisions of this regulation, have al-
ready met the security standard.
c.  Ensure that all personnel have at least 18 months remaining in service prior to submitting the re-
quest for a background investigation, with the exception of Soldiers in Recovery (see paras 2–14 and 3–
22).
d.  Ensure that request forms and prescribed documentation are properly executed in accordance with
the ASO instructions.
e.  Use e-App and electronic fingerprints to the fullest extent practicable to forward requests for investi-
gation to the ISP.
f.  Promptly notify the ASO if the investigation is no longer needed.
g.  Submit investigative requests for contractor personnel under the NISP through the Director, DCSA.
h.  Only request Advance National Agency Check Status Reports on initial background investigation
requests. Do not request for reinvestigations or fingerprints.
C–3.  Processing investigative forms
a.  To be more efficient, before submitting background investigation requests, commanders, re-
questers, subjects, and the ASO representative should—
(1)  Review citizenship documents of individuals born abroad before submitting initial background in-
vestigation requests. All documents verifying U.S. citizenship will be original or certified copies. A copy of
the documents used to verify citizenship will be uploaded to the background investigation request submit-
ted to the ASO. The ASO representative will upload the documents to the subject’s e-APP before submis-
sion to the ISP. For individuals who are born outside the U.S., enter extra coverage codes on the investi-
gative request forms to require the ISP to validate citizenship in accordance with national standards.
(a)  Acceptable documentation for U.S. citizenship by birth.  Subjects asserting U.S. citizenship by birth
will provide—
1.  A birth certificate certified with the registrar’s signature that bears the raised, impressed, or multicol-
ored seal of the registrar’s office.
2.  Department of State Form FS–240.
3.  Department of State Form FS–545 or Department of State Form DS–1350.
4.  A valid U.S. passport or passport card, unaltered, originally issued to the subject, that is not over
stamped with “The bearer is United States national and not a United States citizen,” or the U.S. passport
does not have “citizen” blacked out.
(b)  Acceptable documentation for U.S. citizenship by certification or naturalization.  Subjects asserting
citizenship by certification or naturalization will provide—

1.  USCIS Form N–560 or USCIS Form N–561.
2.  USCIS Form N–550 or USCIS Form N–570. Copies can be made of naturalization papers for sub-
mission in accordance with 18 USC 1426.
3.  A valid U.S. passport or passport card, unaltered, originally issued to the subject, that is not over
stamped with “The bearer is United States national and not a United States citizen,” or the U.S. passport,
page two, does not have “citizen” blacked out.
(c)  Acceptable documentation for corroboration of legal status.  These documents or any successors
to these forms can be used to corroborate a person’s legal status—
1.  A valid USCIS Form I–551 (Permanent Resident Card).
2.  A U.S. Customs and Border Protection Form I–94 (Arrival/Departure Record) with an acceptable
visa that authorizes employment in the United States.
3.  A valid USCIS Form I–766 (Employment Authorization Document Card).
4.  A valid U.S. travel document issued as USCIS Form I–327 (Reentry Permit) or USCIS Form I–571
(Refugee Travel Document).
(2)  Provide relevant data concerning the subject of the investigation to the ISP. The subject of each
background investigation will provide personal information required by the ISP. At a minimum, the subject
will—
(a)  Provide accurate and complete data as part of the investigation.
(b)  Complete the appropriate investigative forms through e-App and electronic fingerprint capture de-
vices.
(c)  Execute signed releases, as necessary, authorizing custodians of police, credit, education, employ-
ment, medical, and similar records to provide relevant record information to the ISP.
b.  The ASO representative will—
(1)  Use the DISS and other applicable systems to verify whether an individual has an open case or an
existing investigation that meets the investigation or eligibility requirement before submitting a new re-
quest. Do not submit duplicate investigation requests.
(2)  Provide the subject with instructions for completing the e-App and assist the subject as necessary.
(3)  Document efforts to validate and verify the required information where appropriate and maintain
documentation in accordance with applicable record retention requirements.
(4)  Ensure that all documents are completed in accordance with the instructions of the ISP.
(5)  Ensure personally identifiable information on the electronic fingerprints, releases, certification page,
and the e-App matches exactly.
(6)  Ensure the subject of the investigation signs and dates each document submitted to the ISP.
(7)  Ensure the subject of the investigation recertifies the e-App for changes made on any of the docu-
ments prior to submission to the ISP for processing.
