5–16.  Safeguarding procedures
a.  Internal controls will be established to ensure personnel security data, reports, records, and investi-
gative results are adequately safeguarded and access is limited to official duties by authorized personnel.
b.  Authorized requesters of a background investigation will control and maintain accountability of all
reports of investigation received.
(1)  The requester will maintain a record, to include the SF 86, for each subject who has successfully
begun an investigation.
(2)  Any information verified by the submitter, to include local record checks, will be maintained in the
record until final eligibility is determined.
c.  Reproduction, in whole or in part, of background investigation reports by requesters will be restricted
to the minimum number of copies required for the performance of assigned duties.
d.  Electronic reports must be transmitted through a secure system or encrypted email.
e.  Background investigation records will be received and transmitted through the DISS or DCSA se-
cure systems whenever possible and safeguarded in accordance with AR 25–22.
f.  Classified background investigation reports will be stored in accordance with the regulations (that is,
AR 380–5) that govern the level of the classified material.
5–17.  Records disposition
a.  Reports of investigation may be retained by DA recipient organizations only for the period necessary
to complete the purpose for which they were originally requested and in accordance with the applicable
SORN. Such reports are considered to be the property of the ISP and are on loan to the recipient organi-
zation.
b.  All copies of reports of investigation, to include security questionnaires (that is, SF 86), maintained
by the security office will be destroyed within 90 days after the final determination, to include appeals.
c.  Local personnel security records on persons who are considered for affiliation with the DA will be
destroyed after 1 year if they do not affiliate with the Army.
d.  Destruction will be in accordance with Army records management policy and the Army’s approved
records management schedule. Destruction will be accomplished in the same manner as for classified
information in accordance with AR 380–5.
Chapter 6
Adjudication
6–1.  General
National security adjudication is the process the CAS uses to grant national security eligibility (see DoDM
5200.02 for additional information on national security adjudications).
6–2.  Requests for additional information
a.  A request for additional information may occur when questionable behavior, inconsistencies, or
other derogatory reportable activities related to the criteria in the SEAD 4 arise. The CAS may request
additional information directly from the subject through the commander or security manager (see para 5–
11). When feasible, the CAS may issue requests for information in lieu of issuing an LOI or SOR to deny
or revoke national security eligibility.
