Chapter 10
Security Education and Training
10–1.  General
The effectiveness of an individual in meeting their security responsibilities is proportional to the degree to
which the individual understands them. Thus, an integral part of the DA PSP is the indoctrination of indi-
viduals on their security responsibilities. Commanders will establish procedures in accordance with this
chapter whereby the workforce is briefed regarding their security responsibilities. In addition to familiariz-
ing individuals with key provisions of this regulation, individuals should receive a threat awareness brief-
ing in accordance with AR 381–12.
10–2.  Education and training programs
a.  Security education and training programs are required for Army personnel security managers and
other personnel performing personnel security duties. Training topics include DISS and electronic-Appli-
cation (e-App) or their successor programs of record, security orientation, indoctrinations, initial briefings,
refresher briefings, debriefings, termination briefings, travel briefings, foreign contact briefings, and intelli-
gence threat briefings.
b.  For assistance in meeting security education and training program requirements, visit the DCSA
Center for Development of Security Excellence (CDSE) website at https://www.cdse.edu/. The CDSE
website includes personnel security courses, job aids, reference guides, and webinars addressing the na-
tional security process, DISS, e-App, and various security shorts.
c.  Prior to appointment of personnel security duties, individuals will, at a minimum, complete the CDSE
Introduction to Personnel Security and Introduction to DoD Personnel Security Adjudications courses.
d.  Individuals designated by a commander to make interim national security eligibility determinations
(see para 7–3), process reportable activities and incident reports (see para 9–13), or suspend access to
classified information when an LOI or SOR is received (see para 8–4) will take the CDSE Introduction to
DoD Personnel Security Adjudications course.
10–3.  Information security training
Initial security orientation and annual refresher training will be conducted in accordance with DoDM
5200.01, Volume 1 and AR 380–5.
10–4.  Foreign travel briefing
a.  Commanders will establish appropriate internal procedures requiring all personnel possessing na-
tional security eligibility to obtain pre-approval and report foreign travel in accordance with paragraph 9–4.
b.  Foreign travel briefings are conducted in accordance with AR 525–13.
c.  Personnel with SCI eligibility will follow procedures in accordance with DoDM 5105.21, Volume 3.
d.  Personnel will be reminded of their responsibility to enter and exit the United States using a U.S.
passport, report all unofficial foreign travel, and report the use of a foreign passport when traveling out-
side the United States.
e.  All unofficial foreign travel for personnel with national security eligibility will be entered in the DISS.

individual. The losing command will retain a copy of the foreign travel record on file for 1 year after the
