## SUMMARY OF CHANGE

NGR 600-7
Selected Reserve Incentive Programs
12 August 2014
This publication ―
o
Revises the use of Army National Guard Selected Reserve Incentive
Programs.
o
Clarifies incentive requirements for enlisted, officer, loan repayment,
and specialty incentive programs.
o
Adds Program Responsibilities and Management Controls under Chapter 1.
o Consolidates Enlisted Incentives under Chapter 1.
Non Prior Service
Enlisted Bonus, Prior Service Enlisted Bonus, Enlisted Affiliation Bonus,
Reenlistment/Extension Bonus, Student Loan Repayment Program and
Montgomery GI Bill Selected Reserve Kickers.
o
Adds Officer Incentives under Chapter 3.
Officer Accession Bonus, Officer
Affiliation Bonus, Chaplain Loan Repayment Program and Montgomery GI Bill
Selected Reserve Kicker.
o
Adds Specialty Incentives under Chapter 3.
o
Critical Skill Retention Bonus, Active Guard Reserve Title 10 and Title 32
Selected Reserve Bonus and Military Technician Dual Status Reenlistment
Bonus.
o
All Continued Receipt, Suspension, Reinstatement, and Termination of
Enlisted and Officer Incentives have been incorporated in Chapter 1
(excludes Montgomery GI Bill Selected Reserve Incentive Programs Kicker
and Specialty Incentives which contains these requirements within their
section).
o
Continued receipt of incentives.
o
Recipient of an incentive may be eligible for continued receipt provided
they meet the following conditions stated in paragraph 1-21.
o
Suspension of incentives.
o
Recipient of an incentive will be suspended from the program if they enter
any of the conditions stated in paragraph 1-22, if applicable.
Unit
commanders or their representatives will notify the State Incentive
Manager when a suspension condition occurs.
o
Reinstatement of incentives.
o
Reinstatement of an incentive is not guaranteed.
If a Soldier meets the
following requirements for continued eligibility and reinstatement in
paragraph 1-23, subsequent payments will be processed effective the date

--- Page 3 (Original: p.3) ---

the suspension is lifted or on the adjusted anniversary date of
satisfactory creditable service.
o
Termination of incentives.
o
Incentive eligibility will be terminated when any of the termination
reasons listed in paragraph 1-24 through 1-26 apply and that member will
not be eligible to receive any further incentive payments, except for
Service performed before the termination date.
Once declared ineligible,
termination of an incentive will not affect a Soldier’s responsibility to
serve their current statutory or contractual Service commitment.
o
Adds Chapter 5 Settlement Process for Enlisted, Officer and Student Loan
Repayment Program Incentives.
o
Adds Chapter 6 Administrative correction of Addenda, Annex or Written
Agreement (previously Civilian Acquired Skills Program).
o
Adds Chapter 7 Out of Service Claims and Debt Process.
o Adds Chapter 8 Case Management System.
o
Adds a Glossary of abbreviations and terms (previously Annex A - Sample of
Termination Letter).
o
Adds Appendix A References (previously Annex B - Sample of Student Loan
Repayment Anniversary Notification).
o
Adds Appendix B Annexes, Addendums, Written Agreements (previously Annex C
- Sample of Request for Loan Status Confirmation).
o
Adds Appendix C Management Control Evaluation Checklist for Enlisted
Incentives.
o
Adds Appendix D Management Control Evaluation Checklist for Officer
Incentives.
o
Adds Appendix E Management Control Evaluation Checklist for Student Loan
Repayment Program.
o
Adds Appendix F Management Control Evaluation Checklist for Montgomery GI
Bill Selected Reserve Kicker Incentives.
o
Adds Appendix G Montgomery GI Bill Selected Reserve Incentive Kicker
Codes.
o
Adds Appendix H Draft memorandum for Army Board for Correction of Military
Records actions.
