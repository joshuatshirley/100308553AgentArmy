## SUMMARY of CHANGE

AR 140 – 10
Assignments, Attachments, Details, and Transfers
This administrative revision, dated 4 March 2025—
o
Changes “gender” to “sex” per Executive Order 14168 (throughout).
This expedited revision, dated 16 July 2021—
o
Clarifies individual mobilization augmentee participation for dual department of the Army Civilians/Soldiers
(para 2 – 5).
o
Adds assignment limitation into a U.S. Army Reserve unit from Soldiers in the Individual Ready Reserve for
being a two-time non-participant/ unsatisfactory participant (para 2–7b(7)).
o
Adds assignment limitation into a U.S. Army Reserve unit for Soldiers discharged for unsatisfactory participation
(para 2–7b(7)).
o
Adds tenure requirements for enlisted Soldiers (para 2–21h).
o
Adds the status or condition of an Individual Ready Reserve Soldier which may limit reassignment to a troop
program unit or individual mobilization augmentee duty position (table 4 – 2).
o
Clarifies transfer criteria requirements of obligated officers and warrant officers from a troop program unit or
position to control group (reinforcement) (para 4–4b).
o
Adds involuntary Individual Ready Reserve reassignment criteria for individual mobilization augmentee and
contractually obligated troop program unit Soldiers (para 4–11p and 4–11q).
o
Delegates authority to the Chief of Army Reserve/Commander of Army Reserve Command for approval of
retention removal date for certain Army Medical Department officers (para 7 – 18).
o
Removes the DA Form 4935 (Request for Unit Vacancy Fill) processing requirements (throughout).
o
Replaces “bar to reenlistment” with “bar to continued service,” pursuant to Army Directive 2016  – 19 (Retaining
a Quality Noncommissioned Officer Corps), dated 26 May 2016 (throughout).
