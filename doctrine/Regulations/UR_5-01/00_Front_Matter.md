## SUMMARY of CHANGE

USAREC Reg 5-01
USAREC Knowledge Management Procedures and Standards.
This expedited revision, dated 1 January, 2020—
•
Establishes the USAREC Chief of Staff (CoS) as the KM owner for the organization
•
Establishes the USAREC Assistant Chief of Staff, G5 (ACS, G5) as the KM champion for the organization.
•
Defines the role of KM as a key strategic enabler within the G5
•
Realigns KM from the USAREC G3 to the USAREC G5
