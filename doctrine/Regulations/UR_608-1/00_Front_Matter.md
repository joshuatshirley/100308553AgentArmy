## SUMMARY of CHANGE

USAREC Reg 608-1
National Voter Registration Act
This administrative update is dated 13 May 2020
o Replaces the term “voter registration applications” to National Mail Voter Registration Forms.
o Replaces the term “center” with station.
o Replaces the name of the directorate “Plans and Policy” with Programs.
o Added DOD Directive 1244.13 (Implementation of the National Voter Registration Act (NVRA).
o Transferred section 1973gg of title 43, United States Code, National Voter Registration Act of 1993 to title 52,
USC Chapter 205, National Voter Registration.
