Chapter 19
Required USMEPCOM Daily Reports and RZ.
19-1. Purpose
This chapter outlines the daily reports pulled from MIRS, REQUEST, and RZ
19-2. General
The RZ system generates an email to the GC shop and Bn Ops informing that the results are not returned on
the following schedule: 3 days for HIV results, 7 days for DAT results, or 21 days for ENTNAC results.
19-3. Required USMEPCOM and Other Reports (GC shop)
The reports that are required to be generated and reviewed daily during GC shop opening are:
a. Daily UMF 727-E – MEPS Processing List.
b. The SGC is responsible for the completion and accuracy of all Shipper Reports.
c. The SGC validates the REQUEST Flagged Reservation Report to check for any flagged shipper
REQUEST records. The GC shop makes any necessary corrections to the flagged records.
NOTE: A copy of these reports will be attached to the UMF 727-E and maintained either in an electronic
shared/common file or in the recruiting files in the GC shop (current year, plus previous 2 years). All
MEPCOM reports will be maintained with the processing list for each day in the active files for each recruiting
year.
19-4. Required REQUEST and Close-out Reports (Bde and Bn Ops)
a. Shipper Reports. The Bn Ops will provide a projected ship report monthly and weekly by component to
MEPS. These reports are provided to the MEPS Travel Section IAW MEPS policy and/or MEPS SOP.
b. Daily processing and shipping close-out. All Bdes are required to close out the end of day with
USAREC through EEPD, ROC. If the SGC/GC ship confirms a scheduled shipper who failed to ship, the Bn
S3 must immediately report the error by e-mail to USAREC: RZ usarmy.knox.usarec.mbx.hq-g3-
eepd@army.mil.
19-5. Procedures for Completing RZ End-Of-Day Reports
SGCs will ensure accurate and complete dispositions are entered on all processors. The following result
codes (RSLT CODE) will be entered by the GC shop:
a. REQ TEST (Requires additional test) (A)
b. TEST REJ (Failed ASVAB) (B)
c. NOT QUAL (Does not qualify for enlistment) (C)
d. REFUSED (refused to enlist) (D)
e. ENLISTED (E)
f. NO HELP (Applicant qualifies for enlistment, no jobs available) (F)
g. NO CHG (No change from the last status) (G)
h. CONFER (Applicant needs to confer with influencers) (H)
i. OPT NOT POSS (Applicant desired job or option combination not possible) (I)
