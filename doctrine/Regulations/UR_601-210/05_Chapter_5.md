Chapter 5
Live Scan (LS)
5-1. Purpose
This appendix outlines policies and procedures for FBI background checks on applicants prior to processing at MEPS.
5-2. General
Recruiters will not submit fingerprints (FPs) of applicants solely to ascertain if they are morally qualified for
enlistment. Furthermore, Recruiters will not FP themselves, family members (not processing for enlistment)
or other Recruiters. Do not fingerprint FSs pending shipment with the exception of processing errors resulting
in an additional fingerprinting requirement.
5-3. Station Commanders and/or Recruiter Responsibilities
a. FP only those applicants who agree to enlist. Entry of the correct SSN for each applicant is essential.

5-4. GC Responsibilities
a. Ensure all charges that returned on the LS are listed on the SF 86 along with any previously revealed
charges.
b. Do not “No-Go” a projection if LS results are not present when conducting QC procedures.
c. Do not enlist anyone into the DEP/DTP/DS without LS results, unless authorization has been given to
process with DD 369s.
d. When the LS report is used, compare PRID from the LS report with the PRID of the applicant's record
during the QC process.
e. The LS process does not eliminate the FP submission during MEPS processing. Ensure all applicants
who enlist are fingerprinted at the MEPS. The FP results from the MEPS submission are required and will be
automatically populated into ERM folder label "Live Scan Results".
f. To discern the difference between pre-enlistment LS results and the MEPS fingerprint results; two LS
results or two LS RAP Sheet results folders will appear in ERM. The pre-enlistment LS results will have a
date prior to DEP/DTP/DS and the MEPS results will have a date that is after DEP/DTP/DS. The case
closing transmittal heading in the later results will also show HQ US MEPCOM as the submitter.
5-5. Validity Period/Retains
a. LS results remain valid for 120 days. A new LS submission is needed outside of 120 days to enlist an
applicant. If new violations are revealed prior to 120 days, no new LS is required. All new charges or
violations are recorded IAW self-admission procedures. The required DD 369 and UF 601-210.02 are
executed prior to enlisting the applicant.
b. Retention in the DEP/DTP/DS is not authorized for applicants who initially return unclassifiable FP
results.
c. Results and discrepant charges after return of the MEPS submission results. DEP/DTP/DS discharge
procedures for fraud will occur and a six (6) month wait is required to reprocess.
5-6. Live Scan for 6th BDE Overseas Locations (DEP Trips)
a. Overseas applicants are not required to have LS results prior to enlisting into the DEP/DTP/DS.
Capture FPs of applicants with a valid SSN during the trip and submit them. Capture and submit FPs for
applicants who initially enlist with a TIN prior to receiving their SSN, upon issue of the SSN in either Honolulu
or Guam.
b. Applicants who enlist from Guam or Samoa who have an SSN require LS results prior to enlistment.
c. LS for DEP Trips, remote areas of Alaska, and overseas locations (Europe, Japan, Korea) that must
fly applicants to their locations for enlistment and have no face-to-face contact with a Recruiter prior to
enlistment, are exempt from LS results returning prior to DEP/DTP/DS. Recruiters will capture and submit
prints as early as possible in the enlistment process, but results are not required. Locations with a MEPS
(Alaska) will also ensure that MEPS fingerprints are submitted on all applicants.
