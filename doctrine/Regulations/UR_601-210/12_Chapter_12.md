Chapter 12
Army Reserve Specific Processing
12-1. Purpose
This chapter outlines policies and detailed procedures to ensure applicants are properly processed for
enlistment, shipped to IADT, and separated from the AR. (Refer to Figure 12-1 Army Reserve ROTC SMP
Processing Flowchart.)
12-2. Alternate Training Program Enlistment Procedures
a. The Alternate Training Program (ATP) is designed to provide specific applicants the ability to enlist
and attend IADT without interfering with civilian education or employment. In order to qualify for ATP, an
applicant must be a current High School student (CIHS/109) a High School Senior (11S) who has been
accepted to college, enrolled college student, or a seasonal employee.
(1) The AR uses Advanced Initial Training (AIT) course length and MOS density to determine MOS
availability.
(2) Due to AIT course length limitations, vacancies requiring an ASI are not eligible for the ATP, with the
exception of band (MOS 42R).
(3) GCs are reminded that applicants enlisting for the ATP must have a Mandatory Return Date (MRD)
11 weeks from the applicant’s Reception Station (RECSTA) arrival date, not the ship date. The MRD must be
on the Soldier’s MEPS orders.
(4) HS juniors and college students will ship no earlier than (NET) seven (7) days from the completion of
the current school year. HS seniors ship NET (7) days after graduation to allow time for receipt of the HS
diploma or official transcript.
(5) CIHS education verification.
(a) For CIHS the verification must state the following:

12-8. Simultaneous Membership Program
The Simultaneous Membership Program (SMP) is an Army Reserve enlistment program that enables
Soldiers the ability to serve in the Army Reserve while simultaneously participating in an ROTC program at a
degree producing college or university. The two programs work in concert with each other to develop future
Officers for the Army Reserve.  Applicants may elect to process for the Army Reserve or ROTC first with only
a slight variation to the enlistment record.
NOTE: Current TPU members are eligible for the SMP program but are not processed through USAREC.
These Soldiers will be processed IAW AR 145-1.
a. Process SMP applicants as follows:
(1) Refer to figure 12-1 flowchart for processing guidance and additional documents required.
(2) SMP applicants will schedule a BCT training path and will not be scheduled for AIT. ROTC
coursework will serve as their training to commission as an officer.
(3) The requirement for Basic Training (BT) depends on what year of ROTC the applicant is currently
enrolled (MS II, MS III, MS IV), completion of JROTC, or if they have completed IET for the Army or other
service.
(4) ROTC enrollment and scholarships are processed through the United States Army Cadet Command
(USACC).  Scholarship information is used for SMP processing to determine whether the applicant is a
Contracted Participant or a Potential Participant (See figure 12-1).
(5) All SMP applicants require a vacancy load, MOS will be 09R10 for a potential participant, 09R20 for
an ROTC participant, see 12-4.  The number of SMP cadets authorized for assignment to a TPU will be 3 per
commissioned officer.
b. Potential Participants.  “potential participants” are applicants who are not yet enrolled in (MS III or MS
IV), or who have not executed a DD 4 with ROTC.  The letter must detail that the applicant is eligible to
contract with ROTC within one year. These applicants will attend BCT unless they meet the following
requirements: complete BCT or IADT with any service, completed 3-4 years of JROTC and been granted
constructive credit by the PMS, completed ROTC Basic Camp, or are currently enrolled in MS II.
NOTE: High school seniors are eligible for this program with a memorandum from the Professor of Military
Science (PMS).
(1) Potential participants are categorized as priorities 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7 IAW AR 601-210, 9-14.
(a) Third Priority: Enlistment of a PS applicant who is not currently enrolled in an ROTC program. These
applicants typically do not require BCT, but PS BCT rules apply when BCT is a requirement for eligibility.
(b) Fourth Priority: Enlistment of an NPS applicant who has completed 3-4 years of JROTC, has been
granted placement credit by the PMS for entry into MS II or MS III. These applicants are typically non-
