20-2.  Height/Weight Measurement Equipment
a. Height measurement devices will be the wall-mounted type, SECA MDL 222 or SECA MDL 216.
The device must be properly installed and verified for accuracy after installation. The MEPS Medical
Department is to attach a level perpendicular (only for SECA MDL 222) to the wall to ensure the measuring
device is level.
b. The MEPS Medical Department will check monthly that the height measurement devices are still
securely mounted to the wall.
