## SECTION 3:  MEDPERS

3.1.  ORGANIZATION.
The MEDPERS convenes at least twice a year under the joint guidance of the DASD(MPP) and
the Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Health Services Policy and Oversight
(DASD(HSP&O)) and in accordance with the MEDPERS charter.
3.2.  AGENDA.
The MEDPERS:
a.  Provides the Accession and Retention Medical Standards Working Group (ARMSWG)
with guidance and oversight on setting standards for accession medical and physical processes.
b.  Directs research and studies as necessary to produce evidence-based accession standards
using the Medical Standards Analysis and Research.
c.  Ensures medical and personnel community coordination when changing policies that
affect each community and other relevant DoD Components.
