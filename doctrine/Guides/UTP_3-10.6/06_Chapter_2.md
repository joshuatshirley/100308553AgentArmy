## Mission


## MISSIONED MARKET CORPS

2-1. The Chaplain Corps has two components—the RA and USAR—for chaplain accessions. Chaplain
applicants then have two areas of concentration (AOC): Chaplain (56A), which can be in either component,
or Chaplain Candidate (56X), which is USAR only.

## MISSIONING PROCESS

2-2. The DACH provides chaplain recruiting requirements to the Department of the Army (DA) G-1, which
publishes a mission memorandum provided to BDE S-2. The BDE S-2 distributes the mission to CRSs based
on guidance provided by the CRD. Station missioning is adjudicated based on several factors, including
historical production, population density, population propensity to serve, and geographical and social
economics considerations. See the current fiscal year (FY) Mission Memo to confirm which specific
components and AOCs are open for production.

## MISSION MEMO

2-3. The FY Mission Memo is posted in the HSD Operations SharePoint at https://armyeitaas.sharepoint-
mil.us/sites/TR-USAREC-HQ/SitePages/Directorates/HSD/Operations.aspx. Open the Excel spreadsheet
titled “FYXX Mission Memo” and select the HSD SITREP tab at the bottom to see the annual chaplain
mission (figure 2–1). This memo shows the CRD’s annual assigned mission and includes real-time mission
accomplishment data. The dark blue tabs break the mission down by CRS (figure 2–2).
