Chapter 6.
Targeting
6-1. Targeting is part of the military decision-making process (MDMP) used to focus
effects on achieving the commander’s intent. In USAREC, targeting effects are non-
lethal and identify those markets that contain segments with a high propensity to enlist.
Targeting is a command responsibility that requires the participation of both the
coordinating and special staffs. Targeting must support the commander’s plan and be a
reflection of the commander’s intent. The targeting process is a continual effort which is
a crucial portion of the synchronization from National and local advertisement to the
direct engagement by recruiters.  Refer to Training Circular 5-02, Intelligence, for
specific details regarding the targeting process.
