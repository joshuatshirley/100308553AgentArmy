## Confirm Processing

The major functions we need for the Company Commander or 1SG to focus on with GCRc are the Confirm
Processing, Validating an applicant’s record, managing Projections, reviewing and verifying all supporting
enlistment documents are in ERM, and initiating Workflows. Review Appendix A for additional update
capabilities in GCRc. The next chapters will detail the steps required to complete each action.
This process was created to eliminate the need for a 1037 or calling the MEPS to confirm who will be
processing at the MEPS. Even though this is a simply process, it really requires actions on your part to ensure
the applicant is ready to process for enlistment. To ensure the applicant receives the Red Carpet treatment at
MEPS, part of Confirm Processing steps should include packet validation and a review of documents in ERM.
However, your MEPS SOP will outline what actions you have take before you confirm an applicant’s processing.
For example, when you Approve the projection under Confirm Processing, you are verifying that the
applicant’s record is validated levels 3 and 4 and all source documents are in ERM.

If problems are found with the record validation, projection, or documents missing, either postpone the
confirm processing or
cancel the projection. If
you cancel the
projection, contact the
recruiter to make the
necessary corrections
and instruct the recruiter
to project the applicant
again. Once the
projection is cancelled, it
cannot be revived.  You
will need to create a new
projection. It is not
necessary to action all
projections on the
screen at one time, only
those that are scheduled
to enlist. Once you have annotated the screen with Approve or Cancel for each projection, click the Save

did not cancel a projection, the screen will refresh showing only the approved projections. Incorporate this
process to your daily routine and complete prior to MEPS cutoff. If done daily your applicants processing at the
MEPS should be smooth and without incident.
Once you make the selection from the dropdown no other action is required other than to click on Save.
This information is recorded in GCRc for the MEPS GC. The MEPS GC will know that you have reviewed the
record and the applicant is ready to process. This eliminates the need to call the MEPS to verbally confirm
processing of all applicants. When you return to the Select A Person screen you will notice that the Confirm
Processing column is now showing only Approved projections.
