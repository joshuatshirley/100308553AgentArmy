## People

Overview
The People Overview screen will show records that a recruiter or
the center has in the system. This includes Priority Leads, My Records,
Prior Service Leads, Prospects, Applicants, Future Soldiers, and
Shipped. The selected list may be sorted based on your preferences as
shown.
•
Priority Leads are leads assigned to the station sorted by their priority score.
•
My Records are records that a recruiter has tagged for themselves. These records are
tagged by clicking the flag in the last column.
•
Prior Service Leads are records that have been annotated as having some form of prior
service.
•
Prospects are people who agree to an appointment.  Once the contact history is created
and the lead agrees to an appointment, the Lead becomes a Prospect.
•
Applicants are people who were contacted and agreed to process.
•
Future Soldiers are Applicants who have enlisted and have been verified by GCRc.
•
Shipped are FS’s that have shipped but remain visible for 120 days due to update
requirements after shipping. These are records that have been “shipped confirmed” by
the SGC. Phase I and Phase II shippers will be visible here for 120 days after Phase II
ship date.

record could have been updated by several different lead sources.  The Best Time to

Request MEPCOM Data
This link is available for
all Prospect + records and
will allow you to view data
that is available from
MEPCOM and showing in
GCRc. Before you can do a
request for MEPCOM data,
you must have the 680-3A-E
completed and showing what
we call the Gold Standard
Data.  The Submit button
sends a request to MEPCOM
for any information they have
concerning your applicant.
Each submission is logged
with results being
immediately available.
Applicants that have
processed in any MEPS
within the previous two years
will have a record in MIRS
and the information will be
forwarded to your record. If
the applicant record is
currently owned by another
service the check MEPCOM for data will provide you with a courtesy look of the applicants
qualification data contained in MIRS without doing the traditional (SPF change). This feature
allows you to utilize the qualification data to explain Army opportunities to your applicant. This
data can be used to make temporary reservations and schedule them for enlistment processing.
There are two basic searches: Search by SSN: ARISS sends the applicants SSN to MEPCOM,
and if they have any information for the SSN, they will return the data to your ARISS record,
providing the applicant is not processing for another service and/or has not enlisted.  If MEPCOM
has the applicant record but it is owned by another service, the system will request the record
using a Gold Standard Request. Gold Standard Information is:  Applicant name, SSN, Date of
Birth, City of Birth and Gender of the applicant this information must be exactly as contained in
MEPCOM.  If the Gold Standard data matches the applicant record in MEPCOM, they will send
all information they have, (Scores, Medical, etc.) providing the applicant has not enlisted in any
service. When you complete the projection, the e-SPF will be completed automatically for you as
part of the projection process.
