## Recruiter Zone (RZ)

Overview
Recruiter Zone is now the one stop shop for all recruiting activities.  Recruiters will need to
access the web-based application Recruiter Zone on a daily basis to conduct prospecting and
processing of young men and women into the Army.  Recruiter Zone now provides a central
management point for processing records, planning guide activities, and reports to analyze
recruiters’ prospecting efforts.
Here is a summary of the features for Recruiter Zone:
•
Single location to view, contact and manage all records that is integrated with the Lead
Refinement Center (LRC).  The contact history and pre-qualification entries are shared
between the recruiter and the LRC.
•
A duplicate check is performed when a lead is created.  New leads are checked against
other records by name and then email address, or SSN, of phone number, or zip code to
reduce creating duplicate records.  When a recruiter creates a new lead they will indicate
the prospecting method used to contact the applicant and use either Recruiter or
Referral as the lead source.
•
Recruiters can access and project any record assigned to their Company.  There is one
copy of a record shared by RA and AR recruiters.  Recruiters are now able to take a
record from Lead to Future Soldier (FS) without leaving the web based application.
•
Recruiters are able to view all records in the Company when using the Search By
Company feature..
•
Recruiters may now change RSID’s to view and work records within the company
•
A recruiter may also change RSID’s to view and update School Zone for any RSID
within the company
•
An electronic Planning Guide is used to integrate prospecting plans and events.
Recruiters submit their calendar electronically to the Center Commander for review and
approval.  Recruiters now have the ability to view and add events to any recruiter’s
calendar in the Center. The recruiter will receive an email notification of those changes
when their calendar is updated by someone else.
•
A Reminders section is available for recruiters to set reminders for appointments and
events.
•
Reports are provided for recruiters, Center Commanders, and company leadership to
analyze prospecting efforts.
•
School Zone allows the recruiters and chain of command to complete or update
electronic school folders.  School Zone has its own user guide and can be found on the
Recruiting Functions tab on the portal,

Route Map allows a recruiter to plan their face-to-face prospecting effort using the
Google Earth mapping tool.
•
Refer To allows recruiters to transfer records to the Air or Army National Guard,
Reserves, AMEDD, Chaplain, Marines, Navy, Coast Guard, and ROTC.
•
ACE allows applicants to take ownership of their enlistment application from the
GOARMY website.  The applicant can initiate or the recruiter can register the applicant
within ACE and then have their application sent to the recruiter for further processing.
•
COI/VIP allows the input of COI/VIP’s and also input any pertinent data such as
appointments, important dates.
•
Recruiter Zone has a functionality called All Inclusive View. This functionality is the first
phase of a single person view. It provides the capability to see duplicate records in other
Centers, AMEDD, Chaplain, and ROTC. This does not include National guard
records.
•
Pre-Screening link allows the recruiter to capture law violations prior to committing the
applicant for processing.  This is in line with policy when using Live Scan to capture
fingerprints for the completion of the Moral Screening of prospects and applicants.
•
Recruiter Offline Applicant Module (ROAM) is a separate laptop application that allows
Recruiters to check out only applicant records from RZ for offline packet completion.
•
Manage Temporary Reservation allows temporary reservations to be made on
applicants without leaving the RZ/LZ application.
•
Leads have a rating based on the priority scores attached to each record, which will be
called System Rating. Leads will also be rated based on Contact History as the
recruiter updates the contact history which will be called Recruiter Rating. Recruiter
Rating will be updated as the lead is worked. Once the lead becomes a prospect, the
stars will no longer update but will be visible in Results Lists. Action Zone, Quick
Search, Find Person Search, Priority Leads, My Records, Prior Service Leads,
Prospects, Applicants and Future Soldiers Result Lists will include the System and
Recruiter Ratings for Enlisted and AMEDD users. Chaplain users will only be able to
see Recruiter Ratings.
Accessing Recruiter Zone
Access RZ by selecting it from IKROme under My Launcher/Applications.
