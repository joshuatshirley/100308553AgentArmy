## Reports

This section will give an overview of how to access and use the available
reports within Recruiter Zone. These reports are designed to assist in
analyzing prospecting and processing efforts.  This chapter will familiarize
you with the Reports page. For the reports to accurately record prospecting
activities, it is critical that
you log your contacts and
that the Education Code
is set for those leads you
are contacting.  Failure to
follow these instructions
will significantly impact
the ability to fully utilize
the Reports feature.
To review the
available reports, click on the Reports link and select Overview from the drop
down. The Reports overview page will show the reports available and a brief
description of what information they provide. The links are used to access the
report criteria screens. There is a window on the page that shows your Last
Viewed reports.  It shows the report, time frame requested, and selected
criteria.  Use the past reports to compare against current prospecting efforts.
This will assist in identifying your strength and weakness compared to the rest
of the station.   Regardless of the report selection made, you must complete
the basic criteria screen for each report.
Mission and Performance Report
This section provides an overview about how to access and use the
Mission and Performance Report. The purpose of this report is to provide
the recruiter and Center Commander with a tool to measure the individual
recruiter’s performance against the station and command expectations.  Once
you have selected the Mission and Performance Report, you will be required to make a few
selections.  Complete the criteria screen with the information from the individual drop downs.
•
Fiscal Year: This is current the
Fiscal year and not the Calendar
year.
•
Floor Week: This is the specific
processing week to query.
•
Target Categories:  Unless you
are looking for a specific target
category, it is recommended that
all are selected when requesting
the report.
•
Categories Included:  This
shows what categories are
selected.
•
Show Graph:  Provides a visual
display of the report information.
This is the same for all reports.
