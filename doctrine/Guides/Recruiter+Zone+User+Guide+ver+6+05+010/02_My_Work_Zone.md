## My Work Zone

My View
There are 6 major links under the My Work Zone header.  The first link is My View and it will
display the main RZ page.  The Quick Search window allows you to search for any record within
your company.  You can monitor and track your goals and accomplishments for the day under the
Today’s Goal window. You will be able to monitor, locate and update records that support your
stations Mission Accomplishment Plan (MAP) within the Action Zone window.  The Awaiting
My Action window displays selected reminders and appointments that were updated on your RZ
Calendar or Center Commander has placed a required action on a record.  Route Map acts
similar to google maps and provides ability to add directions to Hot Knock. CLT Actions
provides alerts by displaying Company Commander and 1SG remarks from the companies
MET/TNE and FS Log.
As discussed, the My View navigation link under My Work Zone is really the default main
screen for RZ.  My Work Zone has 5 additional links that will take you
to that screen to complete or review information.  Contact Locations
will display those locations where a recruiter has posted business cards
or a good known location to meet potential leads (the old milk run).  Hot
Leads provides two consolidated lists of what leads the recruiter owns
and what the Center owns.  Leads shown here are defined as a lead
that has already been pre-qualified and screened by the LRC, or
SASVAB-Military Intent, or ADHQ leads. ACE Records are records
that have been sent to recruiter from GO Army web site or a recruiter
has registered the applicant with Ace so they can complete their security investigation record.
School Zone (SZ) is an automated school folder and is discussed in its own user guide.  COI/VIP
allows the recruiter to input these records into RZ.   So lets take a few minutes to discuss these
links in detail.

Quick Search
The Quick Search allows for a basic record search by Last
Name, Email,  or SSN. (If unable to spell the last name, put the first 3
letters and then an asterisk (*) to do the search). Since this feature will
check all records in the Center, the Search Company checkbox allows
for you to check for a record within the Company footprint, regardless
of who the record is assigned to.  An expanded records search can be
performed by clicking on the plus sign next to More search options.
The More search options feature provides the capability to
search by action dates in RZ.  When searching for records, terminated
records should always be included.  If the record has been previously
terminated, there is a very simple Revive procedure within the
application.  This will save time since you will not need to create a new
record and will have the previous history of that record when doing
further processing.  Any record meeting the search criteria will be
displayed next to the search box.
Once the results are displayed, access the record and update any
information concerning the record, log contacts, or send them a
personalized email.  Be careful when using the Email link for blitzing a
market audience.   Only use the Email link here to obtain additional
information or to confirm an appointment.
Depending on the search criteria, there may be a few or several
records.  The workspace within RZ will display the first 50 records.  If
the record is not found on the first screen, click on the next page
number or click Next on the top or bottom of the screen to locate the
record.  Most of the headers are self explanatory, but will be covered in
detail under the chapter of People.  But there are two buttons that we
need to quickly discuss.
The Check Out button is displayed under the results.  It is only for records that have a status
of Applicant.  If the record is not in an applicant status, they will have to process the record in RZ
by clicking on the name hyperlink
The Revive button will allow the restoration of a previously terminated record.  If a record is
revived after 90 days, MAP credit will automatically be given based on processing action.

Today’s Goals
The next window is Today’s Goals.  This reflects
today’s prospecting goals and achievements for all
lead generating activities. My Goal automatically
populates once the recruiter’s plan is constructed
under the Calendar link. The # of Attempts and # of

captures your efforts.
Action Zone
Action Zone is the Active and Reserve processing list for Recruiters. This section will
display applicants that are on the floor today through the next five days.  This section also
provides a ninety day view of any one that is being worked by Recruiters from the status of Lead
through Shipper. Clicking on the bar graph will provide a by name list of records associated with
the section. Below is a description of each section individually.
•
On the Floor Today + Next 5 Processing Days displays the records projected for
processing. Each day will display the number of records projected for the given floor day
and will display throughout the day and provide an updated status of each record.   Bars
represent the type of projection, based on color code.  The date is a link that will display
all of the people on the floor for the date selected.  On the Floor and Shipped sections
will display as above when clicking on the bar(s) or date link. The Applicant column,
when selected, will display the Profile/eNSQ screens. Recruiter column displays the
recruiter who is assigned the record. Arrived will display a Y if GC has arrived the
applicant in GCRc or an N if not. It may be blank if not scheduled for floor. Projection
Type displays what applicant was projected for.   Edu is education code of applicant. QT
displays the applicant QT and when selected displays all Line Scores if applicable.
PULHES displays applicant medical results and when selected will display physical data.
MOS if applicable displays job and when selected displays enlistment data. Enlistment
Verify is enlistment date. GC Result Code will display status of applicant’s MEP’s
processing as pertaining to GCRc.  If the applicant did enlist, their Ship Date will be
populated along with the Component.

Hot Leads List
The next window to be discussed is My Hot Leads List.   This list provides two consolidated
lists of what leads the recruiter owns and what the Center owns.  Part of this learning process
requires understanding the value of receiving a Hot Lead. Hot Leads are based on a  Propensity
Model. This model is unique to USAREC and filters leads and assigns a numeric value for each
answer. The refinement process allows the record to accrue points for things like, how much
blueprint information exists about the Lead; how many times the command has been in contact
with the Lead (national, local marketing, referrals, recruiter prospecting, etc.); how long has it
been since the lead was contacted by a recruiter; how long has it been since the lead has
contacted us.  Records that meet the criteria are then sent to the appropriate recruiter that owns
the Zip Code based on the applicant’s address.

Army Career Explorer (ACE) Records
One of the best leads, is an Army Career Explorer (ACE). When an individual completes an
application on the Go Army web site, there will be records in the Center under the ACE Records
section. These applicants are considered hot because the individual has completed some or all of
their enlistment application and is expecting to be contacted by an Army Recruiter.

##  It is imperative that you act on these records immediately.

The only way to remove records from this section is when they become a Future Soldier; no
action is taken on the record for 30 days; or if the record is terminated. If the record meets the
above criteria, the record will remain on the Applicant list but will not be visible under the ACE
section.  If a record is referred using the Verify/Register ACE link from the I Want to Section,
the record will only appear in the section below.
Each column is explained below.
•
Recruiter column specifies which recruiter owns the record. Each recruiter in the Center
will be able to view all the ACE records within the Center.
•
Person Information has three hyperlinks; the Name hyperlink when selected, will
display the Profile links (old eNSQ, this will be discussed later). Details, when selected,
will display blueprint info of the record. An email address may be present which when
selected will open an email in outlook for contact if necessary.
•
ACE Packet Information displays the date the packet was initiated and also the date the
applicant submitted the packet. Sponsor indicates that a recruiter sponsored the
applicant and sent them to GOARMY to fill out their packet. If a recruiter is assigned, their
name will be displayed. If Sponsor is blank no recruiter has been assigned.  ACE
initiated indicates the applicant used the GOARMY website.
•
Scores/ PULHES displays test data attached to record. This data may be physical info if
a physical has been taken, and or ASVAB or predictor scores to name a few; CAST/EST,
ACT/SAT.

COI/VIP
This is a new section of the RZ application that will allow recruiters to input their COI’s and or
VIP’s. These records will be visible within the LZ application also. Now
recruiters can actually give the COI/VIP credit for referrals.

screen will show any COI/VIP’s that have already been added. Recruiters
can sort by RSID, Title, Prior Service, Person Information, Location,
Last Contact Date, and Next Action Date. The Recruiter may also filter by RSID, Title, Prior
Service, and Commitment. From the screen they may also Reassign, Email or Export to excel
the list or individually based what is checked. They may also update or use the Add to Map
function. Lastly they may also Add a new COI/VIP from the screen.  To update a record, select
the name link. The Add to Map function works the same as it presently does in RZ. The Filter
maybe selected to streamline your search.

The Interaction screen is used to provide information on any type of appointments that are
scheduled or have happened previously. This is the default screen until Add Action is selected.
This screen will appear after the Add Action link has been selected.  Select the appropriate
action and date and select next.

Once the Action and Date/Time has been set and Next has been selected, the below screen will
allow the input of other pertinent data. The Start Time and End Time are mandatory. Calendar
remarks may be entered for reminders. A Reminder date and time may be set if desired. Select
the appropriate location, Center, person or select other if neither location will be used. If the
Action date is today or is in the past, a Result block and Remarks block will be at the bottom to
enter the results of the action. After the action has been completed Results and Remarks should
be entered.
If Event was selected as the Action, then that event must already be annotated in the RZ or
SZ calendar.  Click the dropdown and select the Event.  If the event date is past or today, you will
see the Next link to enter the Results and Remarks. If the date is in the future, Results and
Remarks may  be entered by selecting the Edit hyperlink from the Scheduled Actions section
after event has been completed.

Change History is a Read-Only screen that displays changes from the Person Info screen.
Change Date column displays the date of the change.  Change column displays changes from
the Person Info screen.  The From and To columns will display the following:  Person, Address,
Email, and Phone changes.  When a record is reassigned, it shows the RSID prior to change in
the From column and the To shows information after change.  Changed By displays the user
who made the change.
