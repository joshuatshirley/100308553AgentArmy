## Calendar

Overview
This section will provide an overview of the calendar function. The calendar may be used to
develop short, mid, and long range planning for events, prospecting plans, and follow-up actions.
The calendar will allow a two week plan to be created and updated to become a detailed
prospecting plan. The calendar will also allow the recruiter to view any other recruiter’s calendar
within the Center including the Center Commander’s calendar and will allow the recruiter to add
events to the other recruiters’ calendars within the Center.
Click on the menu bar and select
Calendar for the calendar home page to
display.  The default view is the current date.
A different day can be selected to review a
previous day’s calendar or to create a plan
for the future.
Here are explanations of the different
windows:
Printer Friendly Version
To print the calendar, click the Printer-
friendly version link.  This option may be
used to print the daily or weekly view both of
which include the reminders.

Adding Contact Plan Activities
In this section, how to construct a plan using the Calendar feature of
RZ will be discussed.  Add Contact Plan Activities and Add Event are
the two features used to develop the plan.  The Add Contact Plan
Activities feature allows activities to be added to the Calendar. Using this
feature allows you to develop a plan by prospecting method that
incorporates the various types of prospecting (telephone, email, mail out
and face-to-face).
Using the monthly view of the calendar, click on the appropriate date. To add the activity,
select either Calendar from the menu and scroll down to Add Contact Plan Activities, or select
Add Contact Plan Activities located at the top of the Calendar homepage.  This will bring up
the plan to be developed or adjusted.
There are four critical pieces
of information required for each
planned activity.  They are the
Start and End Times, the Type
Of Activity (face-to-face,
telephone call, email, mail out),
the Number of Attempts
Planned for this date and
Recruiter Remarks  A note may
also be included below each
activity.  For example, to include
useful information about which list
is being used to make phone
calls.
Begin by entering the start
and end times for the prospecting
activity under the Start/End Time
columns; select the Activity Type
of prospecting method, Email,
Face-to-Face, Mail Out, or
Telephone Call; and enter the
number in the # Attempts
Planned field.  (This number
should show the number of
attempts planned for the time
selected, and will appear on the
Today’s Goals on the RZ home
page.)  Then enter a note about
this activity.

The Actual Attempts textbox will populate as attempts are logged during the times indicated,
via the Contact History or Schedule Campaign screens, or via the Log Attempt link within the
People tables.  The Actual Contacts textbox will populate when the Contact History screen is
updated with a positive contact entry.

##  Note: If the actual time that you contact an individual occurs outside the

period that is indicated in your planner, that contact will not appear within your
Actual Attempts or Actual Contacts textboxes in the Today’s Goals window.
You may need to modify your planner times to receive the credit.
If additional activities need to be added, click on the + Add one more activity button.  This
will allow the creation of additional rows for logging activities.
For each planned day, enter the number of appointments planned, categorizing appointments
by high school graduates, high school seniors, those who have had prior military service, or other
(those who do not fall within these other three categories.)  After the Center Commander has
approved an appointment, you will see the approval tallied up within the appropriate textbox
within the Approved Appts Made section.
Enter notes that are relevant to this particular date in the Recruiter Remarks field.
You should enter an After Action Report (AAR) at the end of the planned date or any date
thereafter.
To save work, click on the Save to Calendar button; after saving the data, RZ will display the
Calendar Home Page.  If you do not want to save changes, click the <Previous button, which
will return you to the Calendar Home Page.
When you have finished entering activities and events for the day,
click the Submit button on the Calendar Home Page to submit the
plan to the Center Commander for approval.  If you are not yet ready
to submit the plan for approval, you may modify the activities for the
day by selecting the Add Contact Plan Activities link at the top of the
Calendar Home Page, or you may add events for this day, by clicking
on the Add Event link.
After the Center Commander reviews the submitted activities, the
Plan Status will change to Approved or Rejected.  Making any
changes to the plan will automatically re-submit the plan to the Center
Commander.
Constructing Your Event Planner
In this section, how to use the Calendar feature of RZ to add events
and set reminders to the Calendar will be discussed. The Add Event
feature provides the ability to schedule short, mid and long-range events
such as the Annual Training Conference (ATC), or planned Leave. These
events may be recurring or non-recurring. The Other feature may be used
for miscellaneous appointments (medical appointment, child’s soccer game,
etc.). These events may have reminders set and can be designated as
recurring events. Once the events are saved, they will automatically
populate the calendar.

Adding AAR Comments to Planned Activities
Recruiter’s should always put in remarks to better explain the daily plan to themselves and
anyone viewing their calendar. You will see the Center Commander comments when that day is
viewed.  An AAR should be put in at the end of the day to detail what happened or failed to
happen with the plan. This will help the recruiter and the leader(s) to determine what adjustments
to make if the plan was not successful. This could be valuable for future plans. To add AAR
comments at any time:
