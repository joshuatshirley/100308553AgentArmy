## PREFACE

This publication, UTP 3–10.2, Army Reserve Recruiting Techniques, expands on the fundamental concepts
and requirements introduced in UM 3–30, 3–31, and 3–32 and UTCs 5–03.1, 5–03.2, and 5–03.3. This UTP
provides detailed concepts, performance steps/measures, and techniques to supplement core recruiting tasks
performed in support of achieving the USAR recruiting mission. Recruiters can improve on achieving
mission success by adopting and employing some or all these prescribed techniques.

## PURPOSE

This UTP provides recruiting professionals, commanders, and staff fundamental information to conduct
effective network-centric operations by identifying, developing, and integrating influencers and partners, and
providing successful techniques that will enable recruiters to accomplish the USAR recruiting mission. This
publication discusses and defines the roles and functions of USAR processes and environments and the
fundamentals each provides to the recruiting forces. It provides recruiters with a basic understanding of the
importance of USAR networking, an understanding of the USAR market, and methods that support the
recruiting mission. This UTP’s purpose is to provide tactics, techniques, and procedures specific to USAR
recruiting operations. Look for “BEST PRACTICE” throughout the manual.
It is not the intent of the UTP to replace AR 601–210 or UR 601–210. Additionally, this UTP provides a
quick reference guide to enduring USAR programs, benefits, incentives, and resources that recruiters must
understand to achieve the USAR mission. UTP 3–10.2 uses USAREC proponent terms specific to recruiting,
where applicable, as listed in the USAREC Recruiting Glossary at https://armyeitaas.sharepoint-
mil.us/sites/usarec-doctrine/Lists/Glossary/AllItems.aspx.

## SCOPE

This UTP supplements existing UTCs and UMs on performing recruiting functions and tasks by providing
additional techniques and knowledge specifically for use by recruiters tasked with achieving the USAR
mission.

## APPLICABILITY

This publication applies to Regular Army and USAR Soldiers assigned to, and Department of the Army (DA)
Civilians employed by, USAREC providing specific support to the United States Army Recruiting Division.

## ADMINISTRATIVE INFORMATION

The proponent of UTP 3–10.2 is the Commanding General, United States Army Recruiting Command. The
preparing agency is the Doctrine Division, G–3/5/7, USAREC. Send comments and recommendations on

USAREC, by email to usarmy.knox.usarec.list.doctrine1@army.mil.
