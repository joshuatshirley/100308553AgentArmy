## Table of Contents

This manual is written to help you grasp the basics of ARISS GCRc and help you use the application
to its fullest potential.   Get the most out of ARISS GCRc by using all of the functions together.  Here you
will learn how to use the automated tools provided to you and how it will make processing of applicants
faster and more efficient.  Publish complete enlistment packets with the easy one time data entry.  Allow
your chain of command to track production without interfering with your processing.   As part of a
continuing effort to produce a product of the highest quality, we would like to hear your comments.  E-mail
your comments, ideas, or suggestions for improvement to ARISSFeedback@usaac.army.mil.
