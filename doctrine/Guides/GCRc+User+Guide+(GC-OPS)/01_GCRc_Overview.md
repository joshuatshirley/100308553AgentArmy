## GCRc Overview

GCRc is a web based
application which is a state-
of-the-art tool to process
applicants for enlistment.  It
is a virtual office that gives
users access to the
complete electronic
enlistment packet.   GCRc
allows the user to add,
update, and delete
information in the enlistment
application.  It automates
the enlistment contract and
annex forms, completes the
electronic packet (e-packet)
with electronic signatures, provides scanning and storage of source documents and allows the electronic
submission of the enlistment packet to various internal and external agencies.   GCRc sends e-mail
notifications to the Senior Counselor when expected results are not received.
GCRc includes an application called Electronic Records Management (ERM).  ERM will allow you to
electronically QC packets along with filing a complete enlistment packet that includes the supporting
documentation.  In other words we are on our way to a paperless enlistment process.  With ERM there is
no more USAREC Form 794 and no more searching file cabinets or desks to find an applicant’s packet.
GCRc will assist you with processing any applicant and provide anyone in the chain of command with the
capability to review each packet.
Another function within GCRc is the automatic quality control workflows that are placed in the GCRc
Inbox.  The GCRc Inbox is a critical work area for all users. As the name would imply the Inbox is an
electronic holding area of work that needs to be completed.  GCRc utilizes many workflows to save time
in filing, transporting, and logging data from packet sign in sheets. Many of the workflows are linked to MS
Outlook and automate the inbound traffic for enlistment packets being replicated to MEPS for processing
but also offers outbound notification from the Guidance Counselor’s as well.  These workflows must be
processed; there is no a magic delete button that will remove the old workflows.  You will need to take the
time and process those workflows to remove them from your Inbox.  Establishing a routine to ensure that
all workflows are completed daily will ensure your GCRc Inbox stays caught up.  These will be discussed
in detail later in the user guide. When you review this manual you will notice that some chapters are
specific to the Guidance Counselors or Battalion Ops, but most of the information is applicable to anyone
that has access to GCRc.  Even though the other chapters are general in nature, everyone should be
familiar with those functions.
The ONLY way to gain access to the application is through
the IKROME.  Since you are located at the MEPS using a LAN,
you will not have to create a secure VPN tunnel on TIER 1
NIPERNET to access the system.  Attempting to gain access
while connected to a Tier II VPN site will result in a “server not
available” error message.   Users will access their portal page
and select the GCRc option from My Launcher under
Applications. If you do not see the GCRc link contact your BN
IMO.  They will grant you access via IMS.  See Appendix A for
what actions is view only versus your ability to update.

Select A Person Screen
Applicant processing begins with the GCRc Select A Person screen. Since there are many different
users, each with a different level of responsibility, there are a few different views a particular user may
see.  The below details the various views of the Select A Person screen:
MEPS User View (Guidance Counselor):
Although different views
are provided, it is important
that the information displayed
contain the same information
to allow users in separate
locations to communicate
effectively.  To accomplish
the Select A Person screen
“guidance counselor view” is
used across all user levels.
The guidance counselor view
of the Select A Person
screen allows users to query
GCRc for selected records.  A
guidance counselor role can
only see what is in their
respective MEPS and respective SPF.  USAREC can see DAR, DAV, and DAZ records while NG can
only see DAG records. The default view of this window for MEPS guidance counselors is all projected
applicants who are processing for that day for their MEPS.  Projections are sorted by RSID and then by
SPF order DAR, DAV, and then DAZ; additionally, the projections are further sorted by processing action.
The Select A Person is held in memory to reduce the time required between applicant processing and
returning to the list.  It is necessary to refresh the list periodically to obtain new projections sent by
recruiters for processing for the day as they may have made special arrangements to provide special
processing for their applicant.  Remember you will only see applicants that have been projected to your
MEPS and do not have the ability to see processors at other MEPS.  However, if your MEPS process
applicants for more than one Battalion you will have access to those records as long as the projection is
made to your MEPS.

RSID and all MEPS they
process applicants at
retrieving all processors for
the current day.  The query is
run against the RSID retrieving all records processing for their RSID regardless of where they are
processing.

Electronic Records Management (ERM)
ERM is where all scanned documents
are stored.  To create an ERM folder for an
applicant, the record must first be projected
to GCRc.  There are three different ways
documents can be scanned to ERM.  The
most common way is the recruiter scanning
documents using Upload Documents
within RZ, using the Scan function within
GCRc, or system generated or loaded
documents.  Whichever way a document is
loaded, when viewing the applicant’s
enlistment packet, you will see the most
current document.  Documents are
versioned and will be viewed in one of three
different formats.  Documents will carry the
extension of .tif, .pdf, or .xfdl.  To determine
how many of the same document has been
added to a record you will need to look at
Document Status under Process App.
There you will see the date and time each
document was added to the record.  Again,
only the latest document will be displayed in
ERM when viewing the applicant’s packet.
Once the record passes validation,
GCRc sends the applicants Profile
information to ERM.  ERM identifies each
individual applicant by a user Profile.
These Profiles will be the top-level folders
in the ERM hierarchy.  The Profile folder
will contain the following attribute data that
will be inherited by the lower level documents and folders. The naming convention for the Profile folder is
as follows: Last Name, First Name, SSN, and SPF.  Applicants may have multiple profiles.  Different
components of the service would generate each of these profiles.  These profiles will be unique to their
individual component and will have different PRIDs.  Everyone must be aware that there may be more
than one profile created for an applicant if more than one recruiter is attempting to process the same
applicant or if the recruiter has duplicate records for the same applicant on their RWS.  It is very important
to eliminate duplicate records on ARISS.  This reduces the chance of a recruiter projecting an incomplete
enlistment application for an applicant that has more than one record.  This also reinforces the need for
the recruiter to project their applicant for all processing.  The profile is created at with the expectation of
putting additional data into their ERM folder.  If the recruiter has not created the profile the GC will not be
able to view/add information.

A DEP/Accession QC Workflow (DEP QC) is initiated by signing the DD Form 2366 for RA and
the Selective Reserve Montgomery GI Bill for USAR applicants.  This creates another QC
summary designed for the Senior Guidance Counselor to review prior to affecting the enlistment.
•
The BN Ops/NCO QC Workflow (BN Ops QC) is initiated by receiving the end of day code of
“E” (enlisted) or “R”(Reno) from REQUEST and the applicant has been “Verified” by the Senior
Guidance Counselor. This QC is for the battalion operations NCO to check and ensure the MEPS
is doing business according to current policy.
•
A Before Ship QC Workflow (BS QC) is initiated 10 to 30 days prior to the applicant’s ship date.
•
The Ship Counselor QC Workflow (SC QC) is initiated for those applicants that have been
shipped verified in REQUEST.  This workflow is designed for the Senior Guidance Counselor to
review prior to the applicant shipping.

contain the standard naming conventions and descriptions of their uses and workflow routing.
•
The Exception Workflow is designed to capture requests for exception to policy granted to allow
an applicant to continue to process for enlistment.
•
Upon receipt of a USAREC Form 1034-R or 1035-R, the BN Operations or Guidance Counselor
can initiate a Reno Workflow to in GCRc.  The Reno Workflow utilizes a dropdown box
providing the requester with a standard list of Reno reason values and once approved will allow
the applicant to change their job.

Upon receipt of a USAREC Form 986-R, the BN Operations or Guidance Counselor can initiate a
Cancellation Workflow.  The Cancellation Workflow utilizes a dropdown box providing the
requester with a standard list of cancellation reason values and once approved will discharge the
applicant from their contractual obligation.
•
For prior service members, a Grade Determination Workflow will allow for the approval of grade
retention or reduction depending on the circumstances and current policy.
•
The Waiver Workflow is used for Administrative, Moral, and Medical waivers to be approved
within USAREC.
