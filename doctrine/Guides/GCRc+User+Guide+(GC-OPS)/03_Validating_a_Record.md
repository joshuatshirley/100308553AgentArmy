## Validating a Record

Validation allows the user to initiate a new validation report on demand. Normally this would be used
after the initial validation indicated numerous errors, corrections have been applied, and the user wants to
ensure the validity of the packet.  Selecting the Validation link from the main menu will take you to this
screen where you can trigger a new validation report to check the current validation status of the
enlistment packet.
An enlistment projection will generate an initial validation report that is e-mailed to the Recruiter,
Station Commander, Company Commander and 1SG identifying the record errors.  These errors must be
corrected to
ensure your
applicant is not
delayed in
processing their
enlistment.
Subsequent
validations will not
send additional e-
mails outlining the
errors found in
the record.
Remember this is
a logic check of
the record, the
Station
Commander
should have
verified the entered data to source documents during their Hot Seat and the MEPS will again verify the
personal data to the source documents and identify any problems during their QC process.
You can review a records validation from the Select A Person screen or on the Confirm Processing
screen.  In the E Valid column you will notice a “Y” or “N” code for each record.  Since we are only
working those validations for applicants that are enlisting, locate the Action column and locate those
records with a “A”,”B”, “C” or “D”.  These are the projections showing if an applicant is schedule to
enlist or ship.  .
To better understand what the GCRc validation does:
•
Level 1 - Checks only the 680-3-AE and Projection screens and verifies that all required fields
contain data.
•
Level 2A - Validates that test projection information is consistent. This level also verifies the
Recruiter ID, SSN, and RSID match.
•
Level 2B - Validates that medical exam information is consistent. Also validates completion of
medical insurance provider data fields.

Now that you know what
each level of validation does,
let’s look at a validation
report.  Click on the “N” link to
open the report. A copy of this
report has been sent to
Recruiter, Station
Commander, Company
Commander and 1SG.  Once
the Recruiter corrects the
record, you must manually
initiate a new validation.  The
result of any re-validation
does not send an e-mail to
the above listed individuals.  If
errors are still showing, then
the Commander or 1SG must
contact the recruiter and have
them make the corrections.

Validation Report, check the
Generated On date and time.
Ensure they are current.  The
report will identify by level and
screen what errors need to be
corrected.  Have the recruiter
make the corrections.  Once

and re-validate the record.
To re-validate an
applicant’s record,
click on the ERM link
and then select the
Validation link.  Click
on the Initiate
Validation Process
button one time.
Once you execute
the process you will
need to wait a few
minutes to verify the
corrections.  Normally, the record update process will display the new information in GCRc within 5
minutes, but this could take longer depending on how busy the main servers are.
