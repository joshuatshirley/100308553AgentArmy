## G6 Knowledge Document:


## How to Set Up New LiveScan WEBS Service on Your


## Government Computer

1.  Go to the Windows 10 Software Center.  Search for 'Crossmatch BioCore LiveScan
Web Service'.  Click install to begin the download.
2.  Once the software has successfully downloaded, connect your Gaurdian 200
LiveScan device to your computer, and then visit the following website to begin
enrollment:  https://livescan.usarec.army.mil/#login.  Please note that the old LiveScan
devices will not work.  If you have one, please turn it into your S4 for disposal IAW
property accountability policy.
3.  From the home screen, select 'Create an Enrollment'.
4.  Next, select 'USAREC' as your workspace.

--- [Page 2] ---
5.  From the 'Personal Information' screen, fill in all required fields.  Click 'Next' to
proceed.
6.  From the 'Physical Description' screen, fill in all required fields.  Click 'Next' to
proceed.

--- [Page 3] ---
7.  Next, fill in all required fields on the 'Transaction Data' screen.  Please note that you
must select 'Other Employment and Licensing' for the 'Reason Fingerprinted' field.
Never select 'Test'.  Please also note that the SON, SOI, AND IPAC/ALC codes are
autofilled by default, however, the user will be able to change the fields if the need to
print a non-USAREC applicant arises. Click 'Next' to proceed.
8.  Next, fill in all required fields on the 'Identifying Marks' screen.  Please note that
'Type of Search' must be left blank, and that 'Request Electronic RAP Sheet' should be
'Yes'.  Click 'Next' to proceed.

--- [Page 4] ---
9.  Next, fingerprint the applicant in the correct sequence.  Once all prints are captured,
the user will validate that all fields have been filled in correctly.  If there are no errors,
click 'Transmit' to submit.
10.  If you have any questions, contact USAREC G6 Desktop Support at
usarmy.knox.usarec.list.hq-g6-desktop-support-team-mgmt@mail.mil
