# Charlotte MEPS Local Operating Procedures

## Medical Prescreen & DD 2807-2 Processing
- **Prescreen Submission Cutoff**: All medical prescreens (DD Form 2807-2 with medical records) must be submitted through MROADS / Genesis at least **72 business hours** prior to projected physical date.
- **Complex Prescreens**: Surgeries within past 24 months, ADHD history, or documented asthma require 5 business days for Chief Medical Officer (CMO) review.
- **Consults**: Orthopedic and psych consults are scheduled on Tuesdays and Thursdays only.

## Shipper and Processing Timelines
- **Projection Deadlines**: Shippers and same-day processors must be locked in system by **1100 hours** on the day prior to processing.
- **Liaison Office Hours**: USAREC Guidance Counselor / Liaison office operates 0530 to 1800 Monday through Friday.
- **Lodging/Hotel**: Contract hotel check-in cutoff is 2000 hours the night before processing.
