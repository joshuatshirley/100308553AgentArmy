# North Carolina National Guard State-Specific Incentives

## North Carolina Tuition Assistance Program (NCTAP)
- **Benefit Coverage**: Provides up to 100% tuition assistance at approved North Carolina public and private colleges, universities, and community colleges.
- **Statutory Authority**: North Carolina General Statutes § 127A-195.
- **Eligibility**:
  - Active member of the North Carolina National Guard in good standing.
  - Must serve a minimum of two years remaining on enlistment contract or agree to extend.
  - Can be used in conjunction with the Federal Tuition Assistance (FTA) and the Montgomery GI Bill - Selected Reserve (Chapter 1606).
- **Application Window**: Must apply through CFNC (College Foundation of North Carolina) by the established semester deadline (Fall: August 15; Spring: December 15).
